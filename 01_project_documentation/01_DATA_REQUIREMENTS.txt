1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
Nexora solutions is a fictional B2B Software & services company

The company manages potential customers, existing customers, sales opportunities and customer relationships through its sales team and CRM processes.

However, the company is experiencing potential revenue leakage due to missed customer follows-ups, inactive sales, opportunities, poor lead conversion, and weaknesses in CRM tracking

This project aims to investigate these problems using business data and identify their root causes.

2. PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT
The purpose of this document is to identify and define the information required to investigate revenue leakage

It will help us understand what data needs to be collected, why each data field is important, and how the information will be used during analysis.

The collected data will support business investigation, performance measurement, identification of operational problems, and development of practical recommendations.

3. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
The main objective is to identify where potential revenue is begin lost, understand why these problems occur, measures their business impact, and recommend improvements to sales operations  and CRM processes

The project will focus o:

Lead conversion performance
Opportunity stage progression
Customer follow-up activities
Opportunity aging
Expected and actual close reliability
Customer renewals
Sales forecasting reliability
CRM data quality and process gaps

4. EXPECTED OUTCOME
By the end of this project, we aim to:

Identify major sales process inefficiencies.
Measures important business KPIs.
Identify the root cause of revenue leakage.
Analyze business data using Excel, SQL, and Python.
Evaluate CRM process improvements.
Build meaningful power BI dashboards.
Recommend measures business solutions.
Present the findings in a final management report

5. DATA DICTIONARY
The data dictionary defines he fields required for the project's business analysis. it expands the purpose, meaning and expected data of each field
 
A OPPURTUNITY INFORMATION

1.Opportunity_ID Description: Unique identification number assigned to each sales opportunity. Data Type: Text purpose: To identify and track individual opportunities

2. Lead_ID Description: Unique identification number assigned to a potential customer. Data Type: Text Purpose: To connect leads with their corresponding opportunities.

3. Sales_Representation: Name or identifier of the employee responsible for managing the opportunity. Data Type: Text Purpose: To identify opportunity ownership.

4. Opportunity_stage Description: Current stage of the sales opportunity. Data Type: Text Purpose: To monitor sales progression.

5. Opportunity_Amount Description: Estimated monetary value of the opportunity. Data Type: Decimal Purpose: To calculate potential sales revenue.

6. Opportunity_Status Description: Current outcome or status of he opportunity. Data Type: Text Purpose: To distinguish open, won, and lost opportunities.

B. FOOLOW-UP INFORMATION

7. Last_Activity_Data Description: date of most recent recorded customer interaction. Data type: date purpose: to identify inactive opportunities.

8. Next_Followup_Date Description: scheduled date for the next customer follow-up. Data type: Date purpose: to identify upcoming and overdue follow-ups.

9. Expected_Close_Date description: date on which the opportunity is expected to close. date type: date purpose: to monitor closing timelines and delays.

10. Actual_Close_Date description: date on which the opportunity was closed. data type: date purpose: to compare expected and actual closing performance.

C. LEAD INFORMATION

11. Lead_Source description: channel through which the lead was generated. data type: text purpose: to evaluate lead source performance.

12. Lead_Creation_Date description: date on which the lead entered the CRM. Data type: date purpose: to measure lead aging and conversion timelines.

13. Lead_Status dscription: current status of the lead. Data type: text purpose: to monitor lead progression and conversion.
